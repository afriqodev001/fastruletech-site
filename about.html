About us

Field technicians who now work for you directly

For 6+ years our technicians have installed phone, POTS-replacement and network equipment for national carriers and integrators. Now we bring that experience directly to Minnesota businesses.

Why we started working directly

We've worked in hundreds of buildings: back rooms, elevator machine rooms, alarm closets, retail stockrooms. Again and again we saw businesses switched to new phone and line services by providers who had never seen the site.

FastRuleTech does it differently. We look at your site first, recommend what actually fits, install it ourselves, and stay your local contact afterward.

How we work

  • We survey before we quote. No guessing from a phone bill.
  • We're vendor-neutral. We recommend what fits, not what pays us most.
  • We document everything. Photos, test results and line maps you keep.
  • We stay local. You talk to the people who installed it.
Service area

Based in Blaine, serving the whole state

We serve the Twin Cities metro and greater Minnesota, including St. Cloud, Rochester, Mankato, Duluth and Moorhead, plus nearby parts of Wisconsin, North Dakota, South Dakota and Iowa for multi-site customers.
