Business phone systems

A phone system that works like the rest of your business

Cloud or on-site VoIP, chosen for how your team actually takes calls. We set it up, move your numbers over and train your people, and we answer when you call us later.

Cloud phone systems (UCaaS)

Your phone system runs in the provider's data center, so there's no server on site. You pay a monthly price per user.

  • Desk phones, a mobile app and desktop softphones on one number
  • Auto attendant, call queues, voicemail to email
  • Video meetings and team chat on many plans
  • Easy to add users or open a new location

Best for most offices, clinics and multi-location businesses.

On-site systems

A phone server at your site that you own, connected to SIP trunks for outside calls.

  • One-time hardware cost and lower monthly fees
  • Keeps working on your local network if the internet goes down
  • Works with paging, door phones and legacy analog devices
  • Platforms include Sangoma (Switchvox, FreePBX) and 3CX

Best for manufacturing, warehouses and sites with lots of analog devices.

Choosing a provider

We recommend a provider; we're not tied to one

We work with many of the major cloud phone providers and on-site platforms. We'll compare a few options for you on features, contract terms and total monthly cost, and tell you which one we'd choose for your business.

The provider bills the service. We do the design, installation and local support.

Good fit if you…

  • Still have an old key system or PBX that's hard to maintain
  • Pay for analog lines you could consolidate
  • Have staff who need to take calls from anywhere
  • Are opening, moving or merging locations
  • Want one local contact for phones and the network
